About Us

Premium Apparel Manufacturing.
Trusted Partnerships. Lasting Quality.

Ashar Industries is a premium apparel manufacturing company committed to helping fashion brands transform ideas into exceptional products. We specialize in private label manufacturing, OEM, ODM, and Cut-Make-Trim (CMT) services, delivering garments that meet the highest standards of quality, consistency, and craftsmanship.

From ambitious startups launching their first collection to established brands expanding their product lines, we provide dependable manufacturing solutions tailored to every stage of growth.

We believe that great manufacturing is built on precision, transparency, and long-term relationships. Every project is approached with attention to detail, efficient production processes, and a commitment to delivering products that strengthen our clients' brands.

Our Vision

To become one of the world's most trusted apparel manufacturing companies by delivering exceptional quality, embracing innovation, and building lasting partnerships with brands across the globe.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower fashion brands with premium manufacturing solutions that combine skilled craftsmanship, modern production techniques, ethical business practices, and reliable execution. We aim to simplify the manufacturing journey while helping our partners bring outstanding products to market.

What We Do

Ashar Industries provides complete apparel manufacturing solutions, including:

Private Label Manufacturing OEM Manufacturing ODM Product Development Cut, Make & Trim (CMT) Product Sampling Pattern Development Fabric Sourcing Screen Printing & Embroidery Custom Labels & Packaging Quality Control & Global Shipping
